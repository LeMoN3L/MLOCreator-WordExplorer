MLO Creator by LeMoNeL - V1.5.3 Community Beta

- World Explorer with local GTA V map streaming, nearby HD and distant LOD/SLOD.
- Direct building selection into a new interior project.
- Map resource creation, categorized prop catalog, movement, rotation and flags.
- Object and map-light removal exports; collision viewing, editing and export.
- Selective model, texture and YMAP XML export.

- Glass transparency controls and game-texture caching improvements.
- Revised interface, resizable panels, camera controls and loading animations.
- Fixed private-model exports by matching archetype names and streamed filenames.
